# Turnoff

"It was after the feed store."

"There is no feed store."

"There was a feed store. Red roof. Dad bought the salt licks there."

"Dad never owned anything that needed a salt lick, Den."

"The neighbors' horses. He bought them for the neighbors."

"Why would he buy salt licks for somebody else's horses."

"Because he was like that. Slow down."

"I'm doing forty."

"Slow down anyway. It's coming up. There's a gate with a tire on it."

"A tire."

"Painted white. Hung on the post. You'd remember if you'd come more."

"Okay."

"I'm not saying that to be a jerk."

"You're saying it, though."

"I'm saying it. There. No. That's a driveway."

"Do you want me to stop?"

"Keep going. It was further. It was always further than you thought, that was the joke. He'd say we're nearly there and then it'd be another twenty minutes and you'd have to pee."

"I was the one who had to pee."

"You were always the one who had to pee."

"I was six."

"You were nine. I was six. I remember because I had the cast."

"You had the cast the summer we went to Grandma's."

"We went to Grandma's after. The cast came off at Grandma's. Mom cut the last bit with the kitchen scissors because the doctor's office was closed for the Fourth."

"That's not what happened."

"What happened?"

"I don't know. But it wasn't kitchen scissors. You don't cut a cast with scissors."

"You do the last bit. The soft part."

"Is there a soft part?"

"There's the padding. The stockinette."

"You looked that up."

"I looked it up after. Years after. I wanted to know if I'd made it up."

"Had you?"

"No. There's a soft part."

"That doesn't mean she cut it."

"No."

"Do you want the box on your lap or should I put it in the back."

"It's fine where it is."

"It's heavier than I thought."

"It's mostly bone. They told us that. The ash is bone. Everything else burns off."

"I know. I was there."

"I know you were there."

"Is that the store?"

"That's a Dollar General."

"It could have been a feed store."

"It could have been anything. They put those everywhere."

"So it could have been the feed store."

"Pull in."

"Here?"

"Just pull in. I want to look at it."

"Den."

"One minute."

"What are you looking for."

"The roof's not red."

"They'd have changed the roof."

"They'd have changed everything. That's what I mean. I can't tell if this is the place or if I want it to be the place."

"Do you want me to say it's the place."

"No."

"Because I don't know either."

"I know you don't know."

"I'm just telling you."

"Go right out of here. Right. And if there's a gate with a tire on it within two miles, that's it. And if there isn't, we pick somewhere."

"We pick somewhere."

"Somewhere with a view. He'd like a view."

"He didn't care about views. He cared about whether the truck would make it up the hill."

"Then somewhere with a hill."

"Okay."

"Okay."

"Two miles."

"Watch the odometer. Not me. The odometer."

"One point one."

"Nothing."

"One point six."

"That's a fence. There's no tire."

"One point nine."

"Stop."

"That's not a tire. That's a mailbox."

"Stop the car."

"Den, it's a mailbox."

"It's white. It's round. Somebody put a mailbox in it. Look at it. They cut the tire and put the mailbox in the middle and painted the whole thing."

"You can't know that."

"No."

"You can't know that's the same tire."

"I said no. I'm agreeing with you."

"So what do you want to do."

"There's the hill. Behind it. You see the hill?"

"I see a hill."

"Will the truck make it?"

"We're not in the truck."

"I know we're not in the truck."

"Yeah. It'll make it."
